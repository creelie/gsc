27 September 2023 \cite{tb23pre}, and in its journal version in the Journal of
Physical Chemistry \& Biophysics, published on 30 October 2023 \cite{tb23}.
The questions studied here come from that work, and its formulas are examined
in \cref{sec:audit}. We thank him for the idea and for that work.
