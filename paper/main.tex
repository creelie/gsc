\begin{abstract}
\itshape
We prove that a passive causal system without zeros in the upper half-plane
cannot shift every frequency of a band $[a,b]$ by the same phase $\theta>0$
without loss: its mean attenuation over the band is at least
$\theta\,(K(\kappa)-E(\kappa))/\kappa$ nepers, $\kappa=(b-a)/(b+a)$, and an
explicit system attains this. With zeros allowed, one explicit system with a
single zero has the least attenuation at every frequency of the band at
once. For a right angle it is algebraic, its mean attenuation is
$\frac\pi2(\sqrt b-\sqrt a)/(\sqrt b+\sqrt a)$ nepers, and no passive system with this phase
passes more than the fraction $a/b$ of the power at $\omega=\sqrt{ab}$.
\end{abstract}
